Real Job — Eagle Point, OR

New Build in Eagle Point — Drywall Dust Before First Tenant Move-In

🏭 New Construction Protocol

The Situation

A property owner in one of Eagle Point's newer north-side subdivisions near Antelope Road contacted us three weeks after construction completion. The 2,100 sq ft home had nylon carpet throughout the bedrooms and hallway — brand new, never walked on — but pre-move-in inspection showed visible dustiness in the pile and a fine gritty texture underfoot. The builder's cleaning crew had cleaned hard surfaces but hadn't touched the carpet.

What We Did

Heavy dry pre-vacuum — drywall dust at the surface first, to prevent it turning to plaster mud during extraction. Mild alkaline pre-spray (lower concentration than a standard soiled carpet). Truck-mounted HWE at 195°F. Two full passes with extraction. pH-balanced rinse. Dry stroke pass to minimize drying time before furniture delivery the following day.

The Result

Carpet visibly cleaner and softer underfoot within 45 minutes. No grit texture remained after extraction. Drying time 2.5 hours (summer, windows open). Tenant moved in the following afternoon with zero complaints. Owner has since booked two additional new build cleans in the same subdivision.
