COMMUNITY CONTEXT

West Medford, Oregon — The Ranch Home Neighborhood

West Medford (97502) is where much of Medford's working-class and long-term residential character lives. The housing stock running along Garfield Street, Stewart Avenue, and Crater Lake Avenue — and the residential streets between West 8th and the Bear Creek corridor — was built mostly between 1970 and 1995. These are ranch-style homes that were built to last and have often been owned by the same families for decades. Some of the longest-running owner-occupied households in Medford are in West Medford, and their carpet reflects that history — original installations, well-used but not abused, often in better structural shape than their age would suggest if properly maintained.

West Medford has the highest concentration of olefin (polypropylene) carpet in any Medford neighborhood. Olefin was the dominant mid-range carpet fiber through the 1980s and into the 1990s — a period that covers most of West Medford's construction window. Olefin is durable against staining from water-based contamination, but it has two significant weaknesses: it bonds strongly to oily residue (cooking oils, pet oils, body oils) and it has the lowest elastic recovery of any major carpet fiber. This means olefin that's been cleaned with wrong chemistry — alkaline solutions that don't address oil bonding — re-soils faster than almost any other fiber type. We adjust our pre-spray pH and chemistry selection specifically for olefin. This is one of the most important calibrations we make on a West Medford job.
