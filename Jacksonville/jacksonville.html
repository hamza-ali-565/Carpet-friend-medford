Real Job — Jacksonville, OR

A Jacksonville Job: 1890s Home, Antique Wool Rugs, Pre-Event Timing

🏠 Historic Fiber Protocol

The Situation

A Jacksonville homeowner preparing for a Britt Festival event weekend contacted us about two antique wool rugs — one dating to approximately the 1940s, one possibly earlier — in the parlor and dining room of their 1892 home. Both had accumulated years of foot traffic from guest visits and the distinctive red volcanic soil from the Applegate Trail nearby. A previous cleaner had declined to touch them.

What We Did

Dye transfer test on all color zones — both rugs fully colorfast. Fiber identified as wool with cotton weft. Pre-vacuumed both sides. pH-neutral pre-spray at 6.5 pH with 6-minute dwell time. Warm extraction at 140°F with reduced pressure, well below wool's safe threshold. pH-balanced rinse with light neutralizer. Flat-dried 5 hours before event setup.

The Result

Both rugs passed color check with zero dye bleed. The pile depth returned visibly — the homeowner described it as "looking the way they did when we bought them." No shrinkage, no fiber damage. Completed 36 hours before the event weekend. Homeowner booked again for post-Britt season.
