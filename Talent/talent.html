Talent, Oregon — Rebuilding Since the 2020 Almeda Fire

Talent was among the communities most severely damaged by the 2020 Almeda Fire. The fire moved through the Bear Creek corridor at extreme speed, and the Talent area sustained significant residential losses along N Pacific Highway and adjacent neighborhoods. The community's recovery has been active, with new construction and repair projects continuing through 2025.

Talent has a different character from neighboring Phoenix — more agricultural land on its eastern edge (Wagner Creek Road, Talent Irrigation District area), more arts community residents, and a slightly older median home age in the surviving housing stock. This means we encounter a mix of post-Almeda new construction cleans and older homes with accumulated smoke exposure from the 2020 event and subsequent fire seasons.

The Bear Creek Greenway runs through both Phoenix and Talent. In Talent, the greenway area near Talent Avenue connects to agricultural land with higher organic soil content than the volcanic clay seen further north. Carpet cleaning calls in Talent often involve a combination of organic soil, agricultural dust, and wildfire smoke — a profile we address with fiber-specific pre-spray chemistry.

Real Job — Talent, OR

Talent Job: Surviving Almeda-Era Home — Persistent Smoke Odor, 4 Years Later

🔥 Wildfire Smoke Protocol · 97540

The Situation

A Talent homeowner contacted us in October 2024 about persistent smoke odor in their living room carpet. The home on Talent Avenue had survived the 2020 Almeda Fire without structural damage — but windows were open during the September 8, 2020 fire event. The homeowner had the carpet professionally cleaned once in 2021 (standard extraction, no smoke-specific protocol) and the odor had partially returned by 2022.

What We Did

Pre-inspection confirmed odor in living room and master bedroom. Oxidizing pre-spray applied with 10-minute dwell time (longer than standard 8 minutes given the extended bonding period). Truck-mounted HWE at 210°F, two extraction passes, pH-balanced rinse, dry stroke, carpet rake.

The Result

Odor undetectable at nose-level inspection after 48-hour dry check. No smoke smell return through a 3-month follow-up. Extended dwell time and higher extraction temperature (210°F vs standard 195°F) was the key difference.
