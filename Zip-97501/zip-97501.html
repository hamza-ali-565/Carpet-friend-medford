REAL JOB · MEDFORD 97501

97501 ZIP Job — Historic Home, Dual Smoke Season, Original Carpet

🏠 Historic Home🪐 Dual Smoke Protocol97501

The Situation

A homeowner in the historic residential area near Bear Creek Park in 97501 contacted us at the end of their wood stove heating season. The home was built in 1963 with original hardwood under newer (1990s) carpet. The nylon carpet had accumulated both summer wildfire smoke and the current season's wood stove smoke, and the homeowner wasn't sure which smell was worse — the carpet had both.

What We Did

Fiber pre-inspection: nylon, 1990s installation, backing intact. No pet contamination via UV. Sniff test at the fiber showed the wood stove character (phenolic, campfire) more prominent than the wildfire character (acrid, chemical). Primary pre-spray: phenolic-targeting specialty formula, with an oxidizing formula on the south-facing bedroom where PM2.5 accumulation was highest. Extended 10-minute dwell, 195°F HWE, two passes in the living room, single pass elsewhere, pH-balanced rinse, carpet rake.

The Result

Both smoke characters were addressed in a single treatment. The homeowner reported odor-neutral carpet at the 48-hour check and again at the 30-day follow-up. The key was identifying which protocol to lead with.
