📦 Move-Out Protocol + Pet Contamination

The Situation

A White City property manager managing units along Agate Road contacted us after a 3-year tenant vacated with two dogs. The 900 sq ft carpet area (two bedrooms and hallway) showed visible staining in the hallway and pet traffic lanes. UV inspection confirmed contamination in the master bedroom closet corner — an area the tenant claimed was clean. Property manager needed documentation for a partial deposit offset.

What We Did

Enzyme pre-treatment on all UV-confirmed contamination zones with 15-minute dwell time. Standard HWE at 195°F for the rest of the unit. Sub-surface extraction with water claw tool on the master bedroom closet corner where contamination had reached the pad. pH-balanced rinse throughout. Provided dated service receipt documenting all treatment areas.

The Result

UV reinspection passed after drying. Zero odor complaints from incoming tenant through 6 months of follow-up. Service receipt accepted by the departing tenant's insurance provider for the partial deposit offset. Property manager now books us for all unit turnovers.
