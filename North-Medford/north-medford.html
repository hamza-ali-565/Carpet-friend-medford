REAL JOB · NORTH MEDFORD · 97501

North Medford Job — Wood Stove Home, 5-Season Smoke Accumulation

🪐 Wood Stove Smoke Protocol🌿 Natural Fiber Rug97501

The Situation

A North Medford homeowner on Columbus Avenue contacted us after the end of their heating season. They use a wood insert stove as their primary heat source and hadn't had carpet professionally cleaned since 2019. The living room had a persistent campfire-character odor they'd normalized over the winters. They also had a 30-year-old Persian area rug in the living room they were concerned about having cleaned.

What We Did

Pre-inspection identified the living room carpet as quality nylon (mid-1990s installation, still structurally sound). The Persian rug was wool-cotton blend, dye transfer tested before any chemistry contact — all colorfast. Carpet: specialty deodorizing pre-spray (phenolic compound target), 10-minute dwell, 195°F HWE, two passes, pH-balanced rinse, carpet rake. Persian rug: pH-neutral pre-spray at pH 6.5, 6-minute dwell, 140°F extraction (lower temperature to protect wool), pH-neutral rinse, flat dry.

The Result

Living room carpet was odor-neutral at the 48-hour check — the client noted it "smelled like it did when we moved in." Persian rug colors brightened visibly with accumulated smoke residue removed from the fiber surface. No dye transfer, no shrinkage.
