Results May Vary

The carpet cleaning results described and shown on this website reflect actual jobs completed by Carpet Friend Medford in Medford, Oregon and Jackson County. They are accurate representations of what we achieved on those specific jobs.

Results in your home will depend on:

  • The type of carpet fiber (nylon, polyester, olefin, wool, etc.)
  • The age and condition of the carpet
  • How long contamination has been present
  • The depth of staining, odor, or particulate
  • Prior cleaning attempts or treatments

We tell you honestly during our pre-inspection walk-through what we expect to achieve on your specific carpet before we begin. We do not guarantee results beyond what we've assessed together at the start of the job.
