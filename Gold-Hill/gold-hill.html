What Gold Hill Carpets Deal With

🌾 Rural Acreage & Agricultural Soil

Gold Hill's character is rural — acreage properties along Lewis Road, Foots Creek Road, and the Rogue River canyon are working farms and hobby ranches. 🎣 Rogue River Access

The Rogue River runs through Gold Hill, and river access points, fishing areas, and the Gold Hill boat launch create consistent riverside soil tracking. Real Job — Gold Hill, OR

Gold Hill Job: Acreage Property — Agricultural Soil & Wool Rug

🌾 Rural Property Protocol · 97525

The Situation

A Gold Hill homeowner on Foots Creek Road contacted us about their 9×12 hand-knotted wool rug in the living room of their 1965 farmhouse, on 5 acres with horses and dogs. The rug had heavy soil from 8 years of agricultural household foot traffic, was visibly matted, and had a faint animal odor. Two local companies had declined the job when they saw the wool construction.

What We Did

Dye transfer test — all colorfast. Fiber confirmed wool pile / cotton weft. Heavy pre-vacuum both sides (two passes given higher agricultural soil volume). pH-neutral pre-spray at 6.5 pH targeting agricultural organic content and animal protein. 12-minute dwell time. Warm extraction at 140°F. pH-balanced rinse with light neutralizer. Flat dry — 6 hours given rug density and October humidity.

The Result

Pile lifted to pre-soil appearance. Agricultural odor fully eliminated. Colors brightened as red clay from Foots Creek Road was extracted from the wool fibers. Homeowner said it "looks like when we bought it at an estate sale 12 years ago."
