THE REVIEW PROTECTION CASE

Why Carpet Is Your Highest-Risk Cleanliness Category

Why do guests mention carpet more than any other surface in cleanliness reviews? Guests encounter carpet differently from hard surfaces. They walk on it barefoot. They put luggage on it. Children play on it. Dogs (if you allow them) sleep on it. And if it's not fully clean — if there's a pet odor that wafts up with warmth, or smoke particulate trapped in the pile from last summer's fire season, or staining that routine turnover cleaning didn't remove — guests notice it at a sensory level before they consciously register it. Then they mention it in the review.

On Airbnb specifically, a single 3-star or below rating in Cleanliness affects your placement in search results for 90+ days. The algorithm weight on cleanliness is disproportionate — because Airbnb knows guests make decisions based on cleanliness above other factors. VRBO's ranking signals are similar. A two-week poor-review period in high season can cost more in reduced bookings than a year of professional carpet cleaning.

Medford's wildfire smoke season runs July through September — peak tourist booking season for Crater Lake and Southern Oregon visitors. Every August smoke event that infiltrates your STR through HVAC or open windows during guest occupancy is building a PM2.5 layer in your carpet that your turnover cleaning company can't extract. By October, guests who check in after a clean turnover can still smell July's fires. This is specific to the Rogue Valley — and it requires a specific protocol.

TWO TYPES OF CLEANING

Between-Guest Cleaning vs Between-Season Professional Cleaning — Not the Same Thing

Isn't the cleaning service between guests enough?

Between-Guest Turnover Cleaning

What it does: Vacuuming, surface clean, fresh linens.

What it doesn't do: Extract embedded soil, address accumulated pet contamination, remove PM2.5 wildfire smoke from fiber depth, address odors built over multiple guest stays.

Frequency: Every booking gap.

Cost: Lower per visit, but doesn't address accumulation.

Between-Season Professional Extraction

What it does: Truck-mounted 195°F extraction, enzyme treatment for pet zones, wildfire smoke protocol, pH-balanced rinse, documented receipt.

What it addresses: Full season of accumulated guest soil, pet enzyme build-up, PM2.5 from fire season, odors that persisted through turnover cleaning.

Frequency: Once or twice per year (see seasonal calendar).

Cost: See pricing →

Between-guest cleaning maintains the surface. Between-season professional extraction resets the carpet to a baseline that between-guest cleaning can then maintain. If you're only doing turnover cleaning and wondering why guests still mention odor in reviews despite a clean-looking unit — the baseline never got established.

YOUR SEASONAL CALENDAR

The Medford STR Calendar — When to Clean and Why

When should I schedule professional carpet cleaning for my Medford STR?

🍃 April–May — Pre-Summer Reset (Most Important)

Clean before Crater Lake season opens. This is the highest-value professional clean of the year. It removes winter occupancy accumulation, remaining wood stove smoke, and winter pet staying accumulation, establishing a clean baseline before peak booking begins. Book: mid-April to mid-May.

☀️ July — Mid-Smoke-Season Check (Optional, High-Impact)

If your property was open May–June and wildfire season has begun with significant smoke events, a July clean removes the first half of the season's accumulation before the second half of peak season. Recommended for Highway 62 corridor properties or ones that allow pets.

🌴 October — Post-Smoke-Season Reset (Second Most Important)

The end of wildfire season is the ideal time to remove summer smoke accumulation before autumn guest traffic. Shakespeare Festival final weeks and fall color hikers keep bookings active. October cleaning removes summer's PM2.5 layer before it's compressed by fall heating and closed-window conditions.

❄️ January–February — Post-Winter, Pre-Spring (Optional)

For properties active through ski season (Mount Ashland, Crater Lake snowshoeing), this window addresses winter occupancy accumulation and wood stove / fireplace smoke if the property uses supplemental heating.
