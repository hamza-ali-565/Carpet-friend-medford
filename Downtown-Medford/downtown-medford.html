Downtown Medford (97501) is a different kind of carpet cleaning market than any other Medford neighborhood. 📦 Move-Out Documentation🏢 Urban Soil97501

The Situation

A property manager for a downtown Medford apartment on South Riverside Ave contacted us after a tenant occupied a 2-bedroom unit for three years without any professional carpet cleaning. Urban soil accumulation from the downtown walking corridor was heavier than a comparable residential home, mixed with some food-related contamination in the kitchen-adjacent dining area. The property manager needed the cleaning documented for potential deposit deduction under ORS 90.300.

What We Did

Pre-inspection identified commercial-grade polyester fiber, common in downtown rental units built in the 1990s–2000s. Soil was heavy in traffic lanes, lighter in corners; no UV contamination detected. Broad-spectrum alkaline pre-conditioner for urban mixed soil, 8-minute dwell. 195°F extraction, two passes on traffic lanes, single pass elsewhere. pH-balanced rinse, dry stroke, carpet rake. Dated service receipt issued same day, delivered to the property manager.

The Result

Traffic lane soil cleared to a result consistent with normal wear and tear for a 3-year tenancy. The property manager confirmed the receipt met their documentation requirement for the deposit adjustment, and the unit was re-listed within the week.
