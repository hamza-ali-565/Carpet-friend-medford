The Situation

A property owner with an STR on the Highway 62 corridor in East Medford contacted us in October 2025 for an end-of-season reset. The property had been heavily booked May through September and had been through the full July–September smoke season with no ventilation — guests kept windows closed on AQI advisory days. Result: 5 months of guest-traffic soil plus a full summer of smoke infiltration through the HVAC system.

What We Did

Fiber pre-inspection: mid-grade nylon. UV inspection found one unauthorized pet zone in the master bedroom. We led with oxidizing pre-spray for the smoke PM2.5 (deepest-bonded compound), 10-minute dwell on all carpeted areas, enzyme pre-spray on the pet zone concurrently. Main extraction: 195°F, two passes on all areas, pH-balanced rinse, carpet rake.

The Result

Smoke odor resolved at the 48-hour check. Pet zone odor-neutral same check. The owner rebooked for the following October — making the post-season clean a standing annual appointment. Next-season guest reviews mentioned a "fresh, clean smell" in the listing notes.
