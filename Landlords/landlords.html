THE RECEIPT — WHAT'S IN IT

What Our IICRC-Certified Service Receipt Actually Contains

What does your receipt include that other cleaners don't?

Most carpet cleaning receipts contain the amount charged and a date. That's not enough for an ORS 90.300 deposit deduction.
  • Date of service (specific day, not approximate)
  • Full service address (unit number if applicable)
  • Scope of treatment (rooms cleaned, treatment types applied)
  • Technician's IICRC certification number
  • Total amount charged
  • Business name, address, and contact

This receipt format has been accepted by Jackson County property managers, Oregon Housing Appeals Board proceedings, and small claims court filings for deposit dispute documentation. We issue it same-day on completion of every job.

Why Rental Properties in Jackson County Have Specific Carpet Challenges

Do Medford rental properties have carpet problems that don't exist in other markets?

🛠

Volcanic Trail Soil from Table Rocks and Bear Creek

Rental tenants in East Medford (97504) and South Medford who access Table Rocks Trailhead or Bear Creek Greenway daily deposit volcanic basalt clay at rates above standard household foot traffic. Waiting too long to clean after move-out accelerates fiber damage.

🔥

Wildfire Smoke in Rental Units

Jackson County's July–September wildfire smoke seasons deposit PM2.5 into rental unit carpet through open windows and recirculating HVAC. Older White City and West Medford (97502) buildings with less-sealed construction accumulate smoke significantly faster.

High Turnover in Post-Almeda Rebuild Areas

The Phoenix/Talent rental market has seen rapid turnover since the 2020 Almeda Fire rebuild. New construction units from 2021–2024 never professionally cleaned may have construction particulate needing different pre-spray chemistry.
