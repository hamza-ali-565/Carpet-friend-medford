How We Work

The Things We Won't Do

We won't quote you one price and charge another.

Price is confirmed before any work begins, every time. If something changes the scope mid-job, we stop and tell you before continuing. No surprises after the truck is in your driveway.

We won't skip the pre-vacuum to save time.

That step removes 80–90% of dry soil before water touches your carpet — skipping it is the single biggest reason carpets re-soil fast after cleaning. We do it every job, even though it adds 15 minutes.

We won't tell you we can fix something if we can't.

If a carpet is saturated to the subfloor from years of cat contamination and replacement makes more sense than cleaning, we'll tell you that before we start. We'd rather lose the job than take your money for a result that won't hold.

We won't use one enzyme formula for every pet situation.

Cat and dog urine have different chemistry and require different treatments. A generic "pet deodorizer" misses one or the other. We bring both and use the right one per zone.

We won't let you buy a wildfire smoke treatment you don't need.

If a smoke event didn't affect your area significantly, we'll tell you standard cleaning is appropriate. Upselling a $150 smoke protocol on a home that doesn't need it is not something we do.
