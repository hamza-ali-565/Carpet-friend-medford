Rug Fiber Types We Clean — What Separates Professional Rug Cleaners from General Carpet Cleaners

🧳 Synthetic (Polypropylene, Nylon, Polyester)

Most forgiving — tolerates standard alkaline pre-spray and hot water extraction up to 195°F. Dries fastest (2–3 hrs in Medford summer). Main risk is over-wetting a delaminating latex secondary backing.

🧸 Wool Area Rugs

Requires pH-neutral chemistry (wool lives at 4.5–7 pH) and warm — not hot — water. Felts and shrinks in alkaline chemistry or high heat. Extended drying (4–6 hrs flat). We never roll wool damp.

📚 Hand-Knotted Persian, Turkish & Oriental Rugs

The most common searches we get: oriental rug cleaning near me, persian rug cleaning near me, turkish rug cleaning near me, and antique rug cleaning near me. All require a mandatory dye transfer test before any water is applied — natural plant dyes run in alkaline conditions. Colorfast pieces: cold water, pH-neutral pre-spray, gentle extraction. Non-colorfast: dry-cleaning method only. We never guess on a valuable piece.

🌿 Flat-Weave (Kilim, Dhurrie, Cotton)

No pile — cleaned with even, low-moisture application to prevent tide marks. Cotton shrinks in high heat. We apply consistent moisture across the full surface before extraction.

💦 Shag & High-Pile

Long pile traps debris deep and holds more moisture. Pre-vacuum both sides. Slow extraction passes to avoid over-wetting the backing. Pile groomed upright after cleaning to prevent mat-drying flat.

🏖 Silk Blend Rugs

Silk is S-coded (solvent cleaning only in most cases). We test for colorfastness and fiber content. If water-cleaning risks damage, we tell you before starting — not after.

Our Area Rug Cleaning Process — How Our Rug Wash Service Works

1

Fiber & Dye Identification

We read the manufacturer tag for fiber content and cleaning code. For hand-knotted rugs, we do a dye transfer test on each color zone. This determines everything that follows.

2

Dry Pre-Vacuum — Both Sides

We vacuum the top face and the underside. Debris that migrates through the pile to the backing falls back up when walked on. Double-sided pre-vacuum removes up to 90% of dry soil before any moisture is introduced.

3

Fiber-Matched Pre-Spray Application

Alkaline chemistry for synthetic fibers with oily soiling. pH-neutral for wool and silk. Enzyme pre-treatment for pet contamination zones. Oxidizing agent for wildfire smoke events. Dwell time: 5–10 minutes.

4

Low-Moisture Extraction

Truck-mounted system with reduced pressure settings for rugs — enough extraction force to pull contamination without distorting pile structure or over-wetting the backing foundation.

5

pH-Balanced Rinse

Neutralizes alkaline pre-spray residue that would otherwise attract new soil within weeks. Critical step most budget cleaners skip on rugs.

6

Flat Drying & Pile Grooming

Rug laid completely flat — never folded or hung while damp. Pile groomed upright. Fan circulation in summer Medford conditions: 2–4 hrs synthetic, 4–6 hrs wool, up to 8 hrs hand-knotted. We stay until you have a clear drying timeline.
