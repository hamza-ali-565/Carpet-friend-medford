REAL JOB · ZIP CODE 97503

97503 Job — Jacksonville Historic Home, Almeda Smoke + Antique Wool Rug

🏙 Historic Fiber Protocol🔥 Almeda SmokeJacksonville · 97503

The Situation

A Jacksonville homeowner on California Street (a historic district residential street dating from the 1880s) contacted us in April 2025 for post-smoke-season cleaning. The home had original carpet in two rooms and a large antique Turkish wool rug in the parlor. The 2020 Almeda smoke event, plus subsequent annual smoke seasons, had accumulated. The homeowner had avoided professional cleaning out of concern for the antique rug.

What We Did

Fiber testing: contemporary carpet was mid-grade nylon over original hardwood. Antique Turkish rug: hand-knotted wool, dye-transfer tested — all dyes colorfast, no backing deterioration. Rug: pH-neutral pre-spray at pH 6.8, 6-minute dwell, 140°F extraction (wool-safe), neutral rinse, flat dry, manual pile grooming. Contemporary carpet: oxidizing pre-spray for Almeda-era smoke, extended 10-minute dwell, 195°F HWE, pH-balanced rinse, carpet rake.

The Result

Antique rug: colors brightened, smoke odor eliminated, no dye transfer, no shrinkage. Contemporary carpet: smoke odor resolved at the 48-hour check. The homeowner had been told by a national chain the antique rug "couldn't be safely cleaned" — it could. Fiber testing and appropriate chemistry is the correct approach.
