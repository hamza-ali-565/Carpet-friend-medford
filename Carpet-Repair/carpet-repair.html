Repair vs Replace

Fix It Before You Replace It — The Medford Carpet Repair Case

Is carpet repair ever as good as replacement?

Most Medford homeowners who notice a burn hole, a badly damaged seam, or a large pet-damaged area assume the only answer is full carpet replacement — and call us for a cleaning quote on carpet they've already decided to throw away. But carpet repair can address the majority of common damage types for a fraction of replacement cost, and a well-executed patch or seam repair is genuinely undetectable in normal use.

Carpet repair makes the most sense when the carpet is otherwise in good condition (structurally sound, with decent pile height and fiber integrity), the damage is isolated rather than spread throughout the home, and donor carpet material is available from a closet, under a bed, or from a remnant piece. In these situations, repair typically costs significantly less than replacement. See our pricing guide for current Medford repair rate ranges.

We'll tell you honestly when repair isn't the right answer: if the carpet is at the end of its useful life, if the damage is so extensive that repairs would be more visible than new carpet, or if subfloor condition requires full removal and access. A pre-repair assessment is always free.

Repair Types

Carpet Repairs We Perform in Medford and Jackson County

🪛

Patch Repair (Damage Replacement)

The most common repair request in Medford. A damaged section — pet damage, burn, an un-cleanable stain, a heavy wear spot — is cut out and replaced with a matching piece from the same carpet, seamed with heat-bond tape and power-stretched flat. In nylon and polyester carpet, a good patch is essentially invisible at standing height.

🧵

Seam Repair

Carpet seams separate over time — from heavy furniture dragging, improper original installation, moisture events, or age. We reattach loose or separated seams with hot-melt tape and a seam iron, re-stretching and re-tacking as needed. In older Medford homes (1970s–1990s), seam separation is one of the most common repair requests.

🐾

Pet Damage Repair

Jackson County's above-average pet ownership makes pet damage our most frequent repair scenario — clawing at doorways, chewing at edges and corners, localized destruction. Most pet damage is patchable if donor carpet is available. Pet odor contamination alongside physical damage is addressed before patching.

🔥

Burn Hole Repair

Cigarette burns, candle wax drops, fireplace ember sparks, and iron accidents create small, visible burn holes. For single or multiple small burns, patch repair with closet remnant donor material is the standard fix. Larger scorch areas — common in Medford's wood-stove culture — use a larger patch or seam-based repair.

🔄

Carpet Re-Stretching (Ripples and Buckling)

Carpet that has loosened from the tack strips and formed waves is unsightly and a trip hazard. Re-stretching with a power stretcher removes waves and re-tacks the carpet. This is distinct from our dedicated carpet stretching service for whole-room buckling.

📌

Edge Re-Tacking and Transition Repair

Carpet edges that have pulled away from tack strips at doorways, stairs, and room perimeters are unsightly and a safety hazard. We re-tack the edge and replace damaged tack strip sections. Lifted or separated hard-floor transitions are also re-secured or replaced.

What We Can and Can't Repair

Carpet Repair Honest Assessment

Is there carpet damage that's beyond repair?

We don't oversell what repair can achieve. Here's the honest line.

Can be repaired successfully

  • ✅ Isolated burn holes (small to medium)
  • ✅ Pet damage at specific locations with donor material available
  • ✅ Seam separation and edge re-tacking
  • ✅ Carpet that has pulled away from tack strips
  • ✅ Small to medium damaged zones where surrounding carpet is in good condition
  • ✅ Pattern carpet — if the match is viable (we'll show you before committing)

Typically better to replace

  • ❌ Widespread damage across the full carpet area — multiple patches would be more visible and expensive than new carpet
  • ❌ No viable donor material exists and the pattern can't be matched
  • ❌ Carpet at end of useful life, where the patch would significantly outlast the surrounding material
  • ❌ Subfloor damage requiring structural access — a different scope from surface repair
