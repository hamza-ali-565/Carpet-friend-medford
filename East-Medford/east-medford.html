East Medford Job — Table Rocks Trail Dog Household, Accumulated Volcanic Clay

🏔 Volcanic Clay Protocol🐾 Pet Treatment97504

The Situation

A family in the East Medford subdivision off Delta Waters Road had two Labrador Retrievers that use Table Rocks Trailhead 4–5 times per week. The living room and entry hall carpet had visible rust-orange discoloration consistent with Table Rocks basalt clay accumulation — the entry hall was the worst, a dense, compacted clay layer that made the carpet feel stiff. They'd rented a Rug Doctor six months prior with minimal improvement.

What We Did

Pre-inspection confirmed silicate clay contamination throughout the pile, concentrated in the entry, living room traffic lanes, and near the sliding door. UV inspection revealed a pet contamination zone in one corner. Pre-spray: alkaline pre-conditioner at pH 9.5, 10-minute dwell (extended given compaction severity), with manual agitation on clay zones before wand extraction. Truck-mounted HWE at 205°F, two passes on the entry hall, pH-balanced rinse, dry stroke, carpet rake. UV-identified pet zone received enzyme pre-treatment before and after the main pass.

The Result

Rust-orange coloring reduced by approximately 90% in the entry hall, with only a slight residual tint in the most compacted section. Pet zone odor-neutral at the 48-hour check. The homeowner had been considering carpet replacement in the entry hall; the cleaning result made that unnecessary.
